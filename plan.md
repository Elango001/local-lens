We are creating a system that people can easily find the 
ongoing offers of the shop and all .the info are updated by
the shop owner into our system

these are all the requirements 

Business registration- Owners submit business information
Business verification -  Admin reviews ownership or authorization
Offer creation - Owners enter discount, description, dates, and conditions
Offer management - Owners can edit, pause, or resume eligible offers
Automatic expiry Expired offers are excluded from customer discovery
Customer discovery - Browse nearby businesses and offers
Category filtering - Filter by restaurant, retail, services, etc.
Location-based discovery Find offers within a selected area
Offer details- Show validity, conditions, applicable branch, and update time
Reporting- Customers can report inaccurate offers
Admin  dashboard- Review businesses, offers, and reports
